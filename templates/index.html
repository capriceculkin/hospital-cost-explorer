01

Who absorbs uncompensated care

How to read it

Uncompensated care is charity care plus bad debt: care a hospital gave and was never paid for. Bars are the average hospital in each ownership type.

Per bed adjusts for size. % of revenue shows how much it weighs on the budget. Total $ favors big systems.

A state may show only a few bars if it has few hospitals of the other types.

02

Burden vs bottom line

How to read it

Each dot is a hospital. Right means more uncompensated care as a share of revenue. Below the line means it lost money in 2022.

03

Health IT capital

How to read it

Hospitals can report assets set aside for health information technology (EHRs, networks, hardware). Only – hospitals in this view report any.

% of fixed assets shows how much of a hospital's capital is IT. vs total assets plots every reporting hospital with a straight-line fit.

Top states always uses every state, whatever the filter says.
